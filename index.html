What we offer

Supervised play, enriched activities, and overnight care built around the unique needs of little dogs.

  • Doggie daycare

    Indoor and outdoor play for dogs 18″ tall or under. Meet & greet required before enrollment.

  • Overnight boarding

    A full day of daycare, then overnight with Tracie. Drop-off and pick-up at 14 Balsam St.

  • Spaw treatments

    Teeth brushing, bath & brush, ear clean, paw balm, and massage — by appointment.

A safe, fun place to play

Constant supervision, clean protocols, and a homey space with room to run, rest, and splash.

We only accept little dogs 18″ (paw-to-back) or under. Pups must be at least 4 months old with required vaccinations. Text Tracie to book a Meet & Greet.
