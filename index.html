F.A.Q.

Ensuring the well-being and happiness of both your dog and yourself is our top priority at Dogs R Us Collingwood. Please sign and return the waiver linked below to confirm your dog’s enjoyment of our environment and interaction with other dogs.

Waiver Policy

Can any dog come in for dog daycare?

Little dog daycare accepts dogs 18″ height (paw-back) or under. Big dog daycare opens August 10, 2026 — text Tracie for size guidelines and availability.

We have a Meet & Greet process before a dog can come and play. Once the dog has successfully completed the assessment, and you have signed the waiver, then your dog may join daycare play anytime.

What is the Meet & Greet?

The Meet & Greet is an initial interview between the pup, the owners, and Dogs R Us Collingwood. This is to ensure that the pup is comfortable here, the owners are comfortable with their pup here, and also to ensure that the pup is comfortable being handled by us.

Does the dog have to be a certain age?

Your pup must be at least 4 months old and have completed their second set of puppy vaccinations to be accepted into our dog daycare program.

My dog eats lunch. Can he have lunch at doggie daycare?

Yes, absolutely, and rest assured that every dog eats separately, so no other dog will eat his food.

Are vaccinations required?

Yes. We require that all dogs be vaccinated from a veterinarian for Rabies, DHPP/DA2PP (Distemper, Hepatitis/Adenovirus, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), and Bordetella.

Do you walk the dogs away from the playground?

For daycare play, we don’t take dogs off the premises unless they are boarding overnight with us, as we cannot control the environment outside our dog daycare facility. Your pup will run, play on jungle gyms and agility sets, and splash in our pools all day in a supervised environment.

Big dogs: separate walking packages are available (15- and 30-minute walks, plus 5- and 10-walk packages). Text Tracie to book.
