Requirements

Who Can Join Little Dog Daycare

Clear entry rules keep every pup comfortable and the group safe.

  • Size: 18″ tall or under, measured paw-to-back
  • Age: at least 4 months old, with the second set of puppy vaccinations complete
  • Meet & Greet: required before enrolment so your dog, you and Tracie are all comfortable
  • Waiver: a signed waiver is required before joining play
  • Vaccines: Rabies, DHPP/DA2PP and Bordetella from a veterinarian
  • Spay / neuter: at the proper age for your dog

A Typical Day

Routine Your Little Dog Can Count On

  1. Drop-off — Mon–Fri from 7am, Sat from 8am at 14 Balsam St

Good To Know

Little Dog Daycare FAQ

What size dogs are accepted for little dog daycare?

Little dog daycare accepts dogs 18″ tall (paw-to-back) or under. Every dog does a Meet & Greet and a signed waiver before joining play.

What is the Meet & Greet?

An initial interview between the pup, the owners, and Dogs R Us Collingwood. It makes sure the pup is comfortable here, the owners are comfortable, and the pup is happy being handled by us.

Does the dog have to be a certain age?

Your pup must be at least 4 months old and have completed their second set of puppy vaccinations to join our daycare program.

My dog eats lunch. Can he have lunch at daycare?

Absolutely — and rest assured every dog eats separately, so no other dog will eat his food.

Are vaccinations required?

Yes. We require vaccination from a veterinarian for Rabies, DHPP/DA2PP (Distemper, Hepatitis/Adenovirus, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), and Bordetella.

Do you offer overnight boarding for little dogs?

Yes. Overnight boarding is available for little dogs only at $75 +hst for every 24 hours and includes a full daycare day. Drop-off and pick-up are at 14 Balsam St.
