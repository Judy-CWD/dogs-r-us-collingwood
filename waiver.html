Waiver terms

Acknowledgement of Risk

I understand that participation in dog daycare, agility classes, training, other pet-related activities or events at Dogs R Us Collingwood, and overnight stays at Tracie’s home, involves risks. Dogs may behave unpredictably, and injury to me, my dog, my representative or belongings may occur despite reasonable care. Risks include scratches, bites, wounds, serious injury and death. I accept these risks and believe the benefits outweigh them. Dogs R Us Collingwood may refuse daycare at its discretion.

Agility Room Rental — Use at Your Own Risk: I understand that renting or using the agility room, including its equipment, is at my own risk. I am responsible for supervising and controlling my dog during rental use and accept the risks of injury or damage arising from use of the room and its equipment.

Veterinary Care & Health

Dogs R Us Collingwood will exercise reasonable care for my dog. If my dog becomes ill or injured, I authorize staff to obtain reasonable veterinary treatment, using my veterinarian when reasonably convenient or another veterinarian if necessary. I agree to pay all related costs. To the best of my knowledge, my dog has not been exposed to rabies, distemper or another communicable disease within the previous 30 days. I will promptly report exposure or illness and will not bring my dog while showing potentially harmful symptoms, including vomiting, diarrhea, lethargy or red eyes, or until sufficiently recovered as determined by a licensed veterinarian. I confirm I am my dog’s sole owner, free of liens or encumbrances.

Photos & Media

I authorize Dogs R Us Collingwood to photograph my dog and use the images on its website, social media, promotional materials and merchandise without compensation. I waive claims related to such use.

Release & Indemnity

I release Dogs R Us Collingwood, its staff and volunteers from liability for injury or damage to me, my representative, my pets or my belongings during any pet-related class, event, service or activity on the premises, including rental or use of the agility room and its equipment. I agree to hold harmless and indemnify instructors, employees, assistants, volunteers, participants, agents and others present against claims brought by me or anyone representing or accompanying me arising from such injury or damage. If I abandon my dog, Dogs R Us Collingwood may take the dog to a shelter of its choice.

Policies

Fleas: Flea and heartworm prevention is requested. Dogs with fleas are not permitted and must be picked up immediately if fleas are found. Clothing/tags: No coats, bandanas or boots; only a cloth or nylon easy-release collar without tags may be worn. Cancellations: At least 24 hours’ notice is required; no refund for less than 24 hours’ notice. Pickup: Authorized persons only. Spay/neuter: Required when age appropriate.
